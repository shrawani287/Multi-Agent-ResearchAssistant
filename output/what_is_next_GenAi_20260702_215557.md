# Research Report: What is Next GenAI

## Introduction

"Next GenAI" signifies the **evolutionary leap** to the next generation of Artificial Intelligence, with a pronounced emphasis on **Generative AI (GenAI)**. This advanced phase moves beyond traditional AI's analytical and processing capabilities, focusing instead on systems that can autonomously create entirely new content and information across diverse modalities (Source: https://www.softvil.com/blog/genai-vs-ai-understanding-the-next-generation-of-artificialintelligence, https://www.codetrade.io/blog/genai-the-next-generation-of-ai-technology). It marks a transformative era in AI, driven by generative models that are poised to redefine human interaction with and leverage of artificial intelligence.

## Key Findings

### 1. The Evolving Core: Generative Capabilities and Advanced Models Defining Next GenAI

The core characteristic distinguishing Next GenAI is its advanced ability to generate novel content, including text, images, code, and audio, often without direct human intervention. This capability marks a significant departure from previous AI forms, which were primarily analytical or predictive (Source: https://www.softvil.com/blog/genai-vs-ai-understanding-the-next-generation-of-artificialintelligence, https://www.codetrade.io/blog/genai-the-next-generation-of-ai-technology). This generation encompasses sophisticated models, such as OpenAI's ChatGPT and Meta's Llama, which are capable of understanding complex prompts and producing creative and coherent outputs (Source: https://www.alignminds.com/next-gen-ai-technologies-explained). Next GenAI is envisioned to significantly augment human capabilities, fostering greater creativity, efficiency, and problem-solving by providing tools that can generate ideas, drafts, or solutions (Source: https://www.alignminds.com/next-gen-ai-technologies-explained, https://www.ibm.com/think/insights/artificial-intelligence-future).

### 2. Enabling the Next Generation: Key Technological Drivers and Enhanced Accessibility

Several key technological advancements are driving the emergence and widespread adoption of Next GenAI. These include the ongoing development and refinement of large-scale models, often open-source, which facilitate broader experimentation and application across various domains (Source: https://www.alignminds.com/next-gen-ai-technologies-explained, https://www.ibm.com/think/insights/artificial-intelligence-future). A significant trend is also observed towards creating smaller, more efficient models, aimed at reducing operational costs and increasing ease of use for a broader audience (Source: https://www.alignminds.com/next-gen-ai-technologies-explained). Furthermore, the increasing availability of pre-built AI models and services through cloud platforms is making advanced AI more accessible to a wider range of businesses and developers, democratizing access to these powerful tools (Source: https://www.alignminds.com/next-gen-ai-technologies-explained, https://www.ibm.com/think/insights/artificial-intelligence-future).

### 3. Broader Implications: Governance, Ethics, and Education for the Future of AI

Beyond its technological definition, "Next GenAI" is also understood within broader contexts that shape its future development and integration into society. As these technologies advance, there is a growing emphasis on establishing robust frameworks for governance and ethical use, exemplified by initiatives like the World Economic Forum’s Presidio AI Framework (Source: https://www.weforum.org/stories/2024/05/next-generation-generative-ai).

In the realm of education, programs such as NextGen AI are dedicated to making artificial intelligence education accessible, engaging, and hands-on for students globally (Source: https://www.nextgenerationsai.com). This student-led initiative offers free access to courses, live Zoom lectures, recorded video lessons, and hands-on AI projects, aiming to equip students with practical skills and confidence in the field. It also guides young minds from coding basics to ethical considerations in AI, referencing the importance of addressing moral and ethical issues raised by AI (Source: https://www.nextgenerationsai.com).

Additionally, the term "Next GenAI" is used in the context of conferences and forums that bring together developers, architects, and business leaders to explore the future of AI and its implications across various industries, such as financial services (Source: https://www.linkedin.com/company/nextgenaiconf). It also encompasses research consortia involved in collaborative efforts to advance AI research and education.

## Conclusion

Next GenAI represents a distinct evolutionary phase in Artificial Intelligence, fundamentally characterized by its advanced generative capabilities to create novel content across diverse modalities. This next generation of AI, propelled by sophisticated models, a trend towards efficiency, and enhanced accessibility via cloud services, is poised to significantly augment human intelligence and drive innovation. Crucially, its development is accompanied by a strong emphasis on establishing ethical governance frameworks and expanding AI education, aiming to cultivate a future generation that is well-equipped to understand and navigate both the immense possibilities and the inherent challenges of this transformative technology (Source: https://www.softvil.com/blog/genai-vs-ai-understanding-the-next-generation-of-artificialintelligence, https://www.alignminds.com/next-gen-ai-technologies-explained, https://www.weforum.org/stories/2024/05/next-generation-generative-ai).
